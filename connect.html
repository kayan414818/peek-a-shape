使用步驟 · How to use

  1. 打開手機／平板的「相機」App，對準 QR 碼。
    Open the Camera app and point it at a QR code.
  2. 點出現的連結，就會在瀏覽器打開立體探秘樂。
    Tap the link that appears to open the app in the browser.
  3. 把這個網址「加入主畫面」，就像 App 一樣方便。
    Use “Add to Home Screen” for one‑tap access.
